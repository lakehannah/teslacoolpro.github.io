## 📊 Content Pillars (40-25-20-15 Rule)

| Pillar | Percentage | Purpose | Examples |
|--------|------------|---------|----------|
| **Education** | 40% | Build authority, teach value | How AC works, maintenance schedules, cost comparisons |
| **Social Proof** | 25% | Build trust, show results | Before/after photos, testimonials, case studies |
| **Behind-the-Scenes** | 20% | Build connection, humanize | Van tours, day-in-life, tools, process |
| **Promotion** | 15% | Drive revenue, CTAs | Discounts, campaigns, urgency posts |

**Never exceed 15% promotional content.** Value first, sales second.

## 🎨 Visual Guidelines

**Brand Colors:**
- Primary: Cool Blue (#2E86AB or similar)
- Secondary: Clean White (#FFFFFF)
- Accent: Trust Gray (#5A6B7C)

**Typography:**
- Clean, modern sans-serif (matches Tesla aesthetic)
- Bold headers, readable body text
- Consistent font family across all graphics

**Photo Style:**
- Before/after contrast shots (dramatic but real)
- Process documentation (hands working, tools in action)
- Happy customers + their Teslas (candid > posed)
- Mobile van/truck branding shots
- Clean, uncluttered compositions

**Video Style:**
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Text overlays (many watch without sound)
- Under 60 seconds (algorithm preference)
- Brand watermark bottom corner
- Clear CTA at end

## ✍️ Voice & Tone

**We are:**
- Knowledgeable (we know Teslas inside out)
- Friendly (approachable, not intimidating)
- Trustworthy (transparent about what we do)
- Slightly irreverent (Tesla owners appreciate humor)
- Educational (empower, don't fear-monger)

**We are NOT:**
- Salesy or pushy
- Condescending or elitist
- Fear-mongering ("you'll die if you don't clean")
- Overly formal or corporate
- Generic (we're Tesla-specific experts)

**Example Tone:**
> ❌ "Your AC is disgusting and you should be ashamed."
> ✅ "That smell when you turn on your AC? Yeah, that's mold. Let's fix it."

## 🤝 Working with Social Media Poster

**Handoff Process:**

1. **Content Ready:** All posts in `/ready-to-post/` include:
   - Visual specifications
   - Full caption
   - Hashtag set
   - Engagement strategy
   - Metrics to track

2. **Graphics:** Created in Canva with brand kit loaded
   - Templates saved for reuse
   - Consistent fonts/colors
   - Exported in platform-optimal sizes

3. **Videos:** Edited in CapCut
   - Brand watermark applied
   - Text overlays included
   - Multiple length versions (60s, 30s, 15s)

4. **Scheduling:**
   - Use Meta Business Suite for IG + FB
   - LinkedIn posted manually (different tone)
   - TikTok posted natively (better algorithm)

5. **Approval Workflow:**
   - Standard posts: No approval needed (follow calendar)
   - Campaign launches: Director approval 48hrs before
   - Sensitive topics (health claims): Director approval
   - UGC features: Get written permission first

**Communication:**
- Weekly sync: Review past week, plan next week
- Slack/DM for quick questions
- Escalate negative comments immediately
- Share wins (viral posts, big bookings)
