# Tesla Cool Pro - Brand Guide

## Unique Value Proposition

**What We Do:** Mobile Tesla AC & cooling system cleaning and maintenance service

**Core Differentiators:**
1. **Mobile Service** - We come to you (home, office, parking lot)
2. **Tesla Expertise** - Specialized knowledge of Tesla HVAC systems
3. **Quick Turnaround** - 30-45 minute service, minimal disruption
4. **Non-Toxic Cleaning** - Safe for passengers, pets, and sensitive systems
5. **Preventive Care** - Avoid costly repairs through regular maintenance

## Target Audience

- Tesla Model 3/Y/S/X owners (primary: 3 & Y)
- Rideshare/drivers (Uber, Lyft)
- Parents concerned about air quality
- Allergy sufferers
- Hot climate owners (AZ, TX, FL, CA)
- Car enthusiasts who maintain their vehicles

## Brand Voice

- **Knowledgeable** - We know Teslas inside and out
- **Friendly** - Approachable, not intimidating
- **Trustworthy** - Transparent about what we do and why
- **Slightly irreverent** - Tesla owners appreciate humor
- **Educational** - Empower, don't fear-monger

## Content Pillars

1. **Education** (40%) - How things work, why maintenance matters
2. **Social Proof** (25%) - Before/after, testimonials, results
3. **Behind-the-Scenes** (20%) - Process, team, mobile setup
4. **Promotions** (15%) - Offers, seasonal campaigns, CTAs

## Key Messages

- "Your Tesla's AC works harder than you think"
- "Clean air = better health + better performance"
- "Mobile service = no dealership hassle"
- "Prevent $2000 repairs with $200 maintenance"
- "Tesla-specific expertise matters"

## Visual Identity Notes

- Clean, modern aesthetic (matches Tesla brand)
- Blue/white color scheme (cooling, cleanliness)
- Before/after contrast shots
- Process documentation (evaporator coils, drains)
- Mobile van/truck branding shots
- Happy customers + their Teslas
