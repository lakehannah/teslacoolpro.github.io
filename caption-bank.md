### E3: DIY vs Professional
Can you clean your Tesla AC yourself?

Kind of. Here's what you CAN do:
✅ Replace cabin filter (easy, DIY-friendly)
✅ Spray vent cleaners (temporary, surface-level)
✅ Run heat to kill some mold (bandaid solution)

Here's what you CAN'T do:
❌ Access evaporator coil (behind dashboard)
❌ Deep clean without specialized tools
❌ Clear clogged drain lines properly
❌ Inspect for damage/corrosion

We handle the stuff you can't reach. You handle the easy maintenance. Teamwork. 🤝
