## 🔧 Common Week 1 Challenges & Solutions (Organic)

### Challenge: Slow Initial Traction
**Signs:** Few bookings, low engagement, quiet response  
**Solution:** 
- Lower initial slot limits (create artificial scarcity)
- Activate personal network (friends, family, past customers)
- Increase community engagement (more helpful comments)
- Be patient - organic growth compounds over weeks, not days

### Challenge: Content Bottlenecks
**Signs:** Not enough posts, videos behind schedule  
**Solution:**
- Batch create (film 5 videos in one session)
- Repurpose one piece of content across platforms
- Use templates for graphics (Canva)
- Start with simpler content (single images, text posts)

### Challenge: Community Rejection
**Signs:** Group admins remove posts, negative responses  
**Solution:**
- Focus on being helpful, not selling
- Read group rules carefully before posting
- Build karma/reputation first, promote later
- Apologize if you misstep, learn, move on

### Challenge: Influencer Silence
**Signs:** 0 responses after 10+ outreach attempts  
**Solution:**
- Revise messaging (more personalized, less generic)
- Lead with value (what's in it for them)
- Try smaller influencers first (more responsive)
- Offer genuine free service, not just "exposure"

### Challenge: Capacity Concerns
**Signs:** More bookings than can handle, scheduling conflicts  
**Solution:**
- Implement waitlist system immediately
- Communicate realistic timelines to customers
- Consider technician hiring/training acceleration
- Adjust future drop slot limits
