## 🚀 Organic Growth Strategy Overview

### The Crumbl "Weekly Cooling Drops" Model

**Core Concept:** Weekly limited-time package announcements create FOMO, repeat bookings, and social buzz.

**How It Works:**
- **Classic Deep Clean:** Always available ($250-450)
- **Weekly Drop:** Limited package announced Sunday 7 PM, launches Monday 7 AM
- **Scarcity:** 10-15 slots per drop, sells out in 48-72 hours
- **Variety:** 4-6 different themed packages per month
- **Social Proof:** Customer transformations shared throughout the week

### 90-Day Organic Goals

| Metric | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **Bookings/Month** | 20-30 | 45-60 | 70-90 |
| **Revenue/Month** | $5,000-7,500 | $11,000-15,000 | $17,500-22,500 |
| **Social Followers** | 300-500 | 800-1,200 | 1,500-2,500 |
| **Email/SMS List** | 50+ | 150+ | 300+ |
| **Active Partnerships** | 2-3 | 5-7 | 8-10 |
| **Ad Spend** | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| **Referral %** | 0-5% | 10-15% | 20%+ |

### Growth Levers (All Free)

**Community Building (40% of bookings)**
- Tesla Facebook groups (15+ groups)
- Reddit r/TeslaModel3, r/TeslaModelY
- Tesla Motors Club forums
- Local Utah Tesla clubs

**Content Virality (30% of bookings)**
- TikTok/Reels satisfying cleaning videos

**Influencer Partnerships (15% of bookings)**

**Referral Program (15% of bookings by Month 3)**

- UGC contests with prizes

**Local Partnerships (Cross-promotion)**
- Detailers, wrap shops, charging installers

**PR & Media (Authority building)**
