## 🚀 Getting Started (Organic Growth)

### Step 1: Assign Marketing Director
The Marketing Director role is the team lead and strategy owner. This person should:
- Have marketing management experience
- Understand **organic community building** (not just paid ads)
- Be data-driven and analytical
- Excel at coordination and communication
- Think strategically but execute tactically
- **Comfortable with slower initial growth** (organic compounds over time)

**Action:** Assign or confirm Marketing Director before activating team.

---

### Step 2: Marketing Director Reviews Strategy
**Time Required:** 2-3 hours  
**Documents to Read:**
1. `EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY-marketing-launch.md` (high-level overview)
2. `marketing-strategy-2026.md` (full organic strategy with drops model)
3. `marketing-director-checklist.md` (activation plan - revised for organic)
4. `free-marketing-activation-checklist.md` (day-by-day first 14 days)
5. All subagent briefs (skim for understanding)

**Decisions to Make:**
- Confirm time availability (80-100 hours/month needed for organic)
- Verify service capacity (bookings/week you can handle)
- Approve pricing/offers (Classic $250-450, rotating drops)
- Approve first 4 weekly drop concepts
- Set up communication channels

---

### Step 3: Activate Subagents
**Format:** Team kickoff meeting (30-45 minutes)

**Agenda:**
1. Introduce team structure and roles (5 min)
2. Review 90-day organic goals (10 min)
   - Emphasize: Slower start, sustainable growth
   - Explain Crumbl "Weekly Cooling Drops" model
3. Distribute quickstart guide and role briefs (5 min)
4. Q&A and clarifications (15 min)
5. Confirm Day 1 priorities (10 min)

**Preparation:**
- Ensure all subagents have access to required tools (all free tier)
- Confirm communication channel set up (Slack/chat)
- Have dashboards ready for Day 1 reporting
- Prepare Drop #1 announcement materials

---

### Step 4: Execute Week 1 Plan (Organic)
Follow the `free-marketing-activation-checklist.md` day-by-day plan.

**Key Milestones:**
- Day 1-2: Profiles optimized, communities joined, content bank started
- Day 3-4: Content creation continues, influencer outreach begins
- Day 5: **Drop #1 announced** (Sunday 7 PM)
- Day 6: **Drop #1 launches** (Monday 7 AM)
- Day 7-10: First bookings flow in, momentum builds
- Day 14: Week 2 review, Drop #2-3 executed

**Expected Results:**
- Week 1: 5-10 bookings (building momentum)
- Week 2: 8-15 bookings (growing traction)
- Month 1: 20-30 bookings total (compound growth)

## 🔄 Operating Rhythm

### Daily
- **9:00 AM:** Daily standup (async or 15-min sync)
- **9:00 AM:** Daily dashboard published
- **All Day:** Subagents execute priorities
  - Community engagement (no spam!)
  - Content creation and posting
  - Partnership outreach
  - Drop slot monitoring
- **4:30 PM:** End-of-day progress check (as needed)

### Weekly
- **Monday 10:00 AM:** Strategy session (60 min)
  - Drop performance analysis
  - Community response review
  - Partnership pipeline check
- **Monday 9:30 AM:** Weekly report distributed
- **Sunday 7:00 PM:** Weekly drop announcement (all subagents support)

### Monthly
- **First Week:** Monthly deep-dive (90-120 min)
  - Full quarter performance review
  - Reinvestment decisions (how to use first revenue)
  - Decision: Continue organic or add paid ads?
- **5th of Month:** Monthly report complete
- **Mid-Month:** Drop concept refresh
