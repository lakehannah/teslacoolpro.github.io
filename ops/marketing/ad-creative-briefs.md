## Creative Asset Requirements

### For Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)

**Quantity Needed:** 10-15 images  
**Dimensions:** 
- Feed: 1080x1080 (1:1 square)
- Stories/Reels: 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical)

**Shot List:**
1. **Before/After Split:** Dirty radiator left, clean radiator right (high contrast)

4. **Happy Customer:** Customer with their clean Tesla (smiling, thumbs up)
5. **Close-Up Detail:** Clogged radiator close-up (show the gross stuff)
6. **Clean Radiator Close-Up:** Sparkling clean coils (satisfying shot)
7. **Team Photo:** Friendly technicians in branded apparel
8. **Tool Shot:** Professional equipment laid out neatly
9. **Tesla Lineup:** Multiple Teslas serviced (show volume/expertise)
10. **Utah Context:** Tesla with Utah landscape/mountains in background

**Text Overlay Guidelines:**
- Max 20% text on image (Meta rule)
- Use bold, readable fonts
- Key messages: "$250 Flash Sale", "Save $1,000+", "Mobile Service", "4.9★ Rating"

#### Video Ads (15-30 seconds)
**Quantity Needed:** 3-5 short videos  
**Dimensions:** 
- Feed: 1080x1080 or 1080x1350 (4:5)
- Stories/Reels: 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical)

**Video Concepts:**

**Video #1: "The Gross Truth" (15 sec)**
- 0-3 sec: Hook - "What's hiding in your Tesla's radiator?"
- 3-10 sec: Time-lapse of dirty radiator being cleaned (satisfying pressure wash)
- 10-15 sec: Clean radiator reveal + CTA "Book now - $250 flash sale"
- Music: Upbeat, energetic
- Text overlays throughout

**Video #2: "Mobile Service Magic" (30 sec)**
- 0-5 sec: Problem - Tesla owner looking frustrated at AC controls
- 5-15 sec: Solution - Mobile van arrives, technician sets up
- 15-25 sec: Process - Quick cuts of professional cleaning (bumper off, cleaning, testing)
- 25-30 sec: Result - Happy customer, cold AC, CTA with phone number
- Music: Friendly, trustworthy vibe

**Video #3: "Before You Go to Tesla Service" (20 sec)**
- 0-5 sec: "Tesla wants $1,200 for a new radiator?"
- 5-10 sec: "We'll clean yours for $250"
- 10-15 sec: Show before/after comparison
- 15-20 sec: "Same result. Fraction of cost. Book now."
- Music: Slightly irreverent, clever

**Video #4: "Utah Heat Survival" (15 sec)**
- 0-3 sec: Thermometer hitting 100°F+
- 3-8 sec: Tesla AC struggling, range dropping on screen
- 8-12 sec: Our service saves the day
- 12-15 sec: "Don't suffer. Book today."
- Music: Urgent, dramatic

**Video #5: "Customer Reaction" (20 sec)**
- 0-5 sec: Technician shows customer the dirty radiator
- 5-10 sec: Customer reaction (shock/disgust)
- 10-15 sec: Show clean radiator
- 15-20 sec: Customer testimonial soundbite + CTA
- Music: Authentic, real moments

**Video Production Notes:**
- Shoot in good natural light when possible
- Use gimbal/stabilizer for smooth shots
- Capture B-roll of: equipment, water spraying, debris falling, technician hands at work
- Get signed release forms from any customers featured
- Add captions/subtitles (85% of Meta videos watched without sound)

## Do's and Don'ts

### DO:
✅ Use real photos from actual services (authenticity wins)  
✅ Show before/after contrast clearly  
✅ Highlight savings vs. dealership ($1,000+)  
✅ Emphasize mobile convenience  
✅ Include customer testimonials when possible  
✅ Use Utah-specific context (landscapes, heat, dust)  
✅ Test multiple creative variations  
✅ Keep text on images under 20% (Meta rule)  
✅ Add captions to all videos  

### DON'T:
❌ Imply official Tesla partnership (trademark risk)  
❌ Use stock photos that look fake/generic  
❌ Overpromise results we can't guarantee  
❌ Forget the CTA (every ad needs one)  
❌ Use small text that's unreadable on mobile  
❌ Make videos longer than 30 seconds for ads  
❌ Ignore brand colors and voice guidelines  
❌ Run same creative for more than 3-4 weeks without refresh
