## NOTES FOR SOCIAL MEDIA POSTER

**Handoff Process:**
1. All ready-to-post content is in `/ready-to-post/` folder
2. Each file includes: visual specs, caption, hashtags, engagement strategy
3. Graphics created in Canva (brand kit loaded)
4. Videos edited in CapCut with brand watermark

**Scheduling Preferences:**
- Use Meta Business Suite for IG + FB cross-posting
- LinkedIn posts manually uploaded (different audience tone)
- TikTok posted natively (better algorithm treatment)

**Voice Guidelines:**
- Friendly but knowledgeable
- Never fear-monger ("you'll die if you don't clean")
- Always offer value even in promotional posts
- Respond to comments like a human, not a brand

**Escalation:**
- Negative comments → Flag to marketing director
- Partnership inquiries → Forward to business development
- Media/press requests → Immediate escalation
- Customer service issues → Resolve within 2 hours

**Approval Workflow:**
- Standard posts: No approval needed (follow calendar)
- Campaign launches: Marketing director approval 48hrs before
- Sensitive topics (health claims): Director approval required
- User-generated features: Get written permission, then post

## EMERGENCY CONTENT BANK

**If we need last-minute posts:**

1. **Customer photo repost** (with permission)
2. **Quick tip carousel** (3 slides, simple design)
3. **Industry news commentary** (Tesla announcement, etc.)
4. **Team member spotlight** (phone photo + bio)
5. **FAQ answer** (pull from common DM questions)
6. **Meme/trending audio adaptation** (stay relevant)
7. **Throwback Thursday** (previous successful post)
8. **Poll/question sticker** (low effort, high engagement)

**Minimum viable post formula:**
- 1 good photo
- 2-3 sentence caption
- 5-7 relevant hashtags
- Clear CTA

Perfection is the enemy of consistency. Ship it. 🚀
