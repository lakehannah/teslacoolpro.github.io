## 📝 How to Join (Best Practices)

### Step 1: Search & Request
1. Open Facebook
2. Search group name exactly as listed
3. Click "Join Group"
4. **Answer membership questions thoughtfully** (see templates below)

### Step 2: Membership Questions (Common Templates)

**Question:** "Do you own a Tesla?"  
**Answer:** "Yes, Model [X] owner in Utah County! Also run Tesla Cool Pro - mobile radiator/AC cleaning service for the community."

**Question:** "What brings you to this group?"  
**Answer:** "Looking to connect with fellow Tesla owners, share maintenance tips, and help the community with AC/radiator care expertise."

**Question:** "Are you a business or individual?"  
**Answer:** "Both! I'm a Tesla owner AND run a small mobile service business helping other owners with radiator/AC maintenance."

**Question:** "Agree to no spam rules?"  
**Answer:** "Absolutely! Here to contribute value first, not spam. Will follow all group rules."

### Step 3: After Approval (CRITICAL)
1. **Wait 24-48 hours before posting anything promotional**
2. Start by commenting helpfully on other people's posts
3. Build karma/reputation first
4. Read group rules carefully (some ban business posts entirely)
5. Set up your profile with Tesla Cool Pro info so people can find you

---

## 🎯 First Week Engagement Plan

**Days 1-2:** Just join, don't post  
**Days 3-4:** Comment on 3-5 posts per group (helpful, no selling)  
**Days 5-7:** Answer questions where you have expertise (AC, radiator, maintenance)  
**Week 2+:** Share valuable content (educational posts, not sales pitches)

**Golden Rule:** Be helpful first, promote second (or never, let your expertise speak)

---

## 🚨 Red Flags to Avoid

❌ Don't join and immediately post "Book now!"  
❌ Don't ignore group rules about business promotion  
❌ Don't argue with members or be defensive  
❌ Don't delete negative comments on your posts  
❌ Don't use generic corporate-speak in answers

✅ Do answer questions genuinely  
✅ Do share before/after transformations (people love these)  
✅ Do build relationships with group admins  
✅ Do offer helpful advice even if it doesn't lead to a sale  
✅ Do let people come to you through demonstrated expertise
