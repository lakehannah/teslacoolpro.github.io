## 💡 Pro Tips from Content Creation

**What Works:**
- Before/after photos get highest saves and shares
- Customer testimonials with specific numbers perform well ("$250 vs $2000")
- Convenience messaging resonates with busy professionals
- Health angle (allergies, clean air) drives emotional connection
- Educational carousels get saved for later reference

**What to Avoid:**
- Fear-mongering ("you'll get sick if you don't clean")
- Overly salesy language (value first, ask second)
- Generic car care advice (we're Tesla specialists)
- Posting without clear CTA
- Ignoring comments in first 2 hours (algorithm penalty)

**Growth Hacks:**
- Engage with Tesla owner community accounts daily
- Share customer stories to stories with location tags
- Use trending audio for Reels within 48 hours of trend emergence
- Cross-post to local Tesla Facebook groups (where allowed)
- Partner with Tesla influencers for service exchanges

---

## 🚨 Red Flags to Watch For

**If you see these, adjust course:**

- Engagement rate < 2% after 2 weeks → Content not resonating, revisit hooks
- High likes but zero DMs → CTAs not clear or compelling enough
- Bookings < 5/week after month 1 → Conversion funnel issue (check booking page)
- Negative comments about pricing → Better communicate value before price
- Follower growth stagnant → Increase engagement with community, not just broadcasting

## 📞 Questions I Anticipate

**Q: "Should we post more than 4-5x/week?"**  
A: Start conservative. Better to post 4x consistently than 7x for two weeks then burn out. Increase frequency once you have a content creation rhythm.

**Q: "Can we use stock photos?"**  
A: Minimize. Real customer cars, real coils, real technicians perform 10x better. Stock only for diagrams/infographics.

**Q: "How much should we spend on paid ads?"**  
A: Organic first for 60 days. Learn what content resonates. Then boost top performers ($10-20/post) to lookalike audiences.

**Q: "What if we miss a posting day?"**  
A: Use emergency content bank. A simple poll or question sticker is better than silence. Consistency > perfection.

**Q: "Should we respond to every comment?"**  
A: Yes, especially in first 2 hours. After that, prioritize questions and meaningful comments. Emoji reactions ok for simple praise.
