## ⚠️ Risk Mitigation

### Potential Challenges

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Slow initial growth | High | Medium | Set expectations, focus on Month 2-3 momentum |
| Burnout from daily tasks | Medium | High | Batch content, use scheduling tools, set boundaries |
| Seasonal demand drop (winter) | Medium | High | Launch maintenance plans, emphasize year-round benefits |
| Capacity constraints | Medium | High | Waitlist management, conservative slot limits |
| Negative review/viral complaint | Low | High | Rapid response protocol, satisfaction guarantee |
| Drop fatigue | Low | Medium | Refresh concepts monthly, survey audience |
| Copycat competitors | Medium | Low | First-mover advantage, brand loyalty, continuous innovation |

### Crisis Communication Protocol
1. Acknowledge within 2 hours
2. Investigate thoroughly
3. Respond publicly with solution
4. Follow up privately
5. Document and improve processes
