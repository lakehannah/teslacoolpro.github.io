3. Blockers/needs from team

**Preparation:** Each subagent sends update by 8:45 AM

---

### Weekly Strategy Session (Monday 10:00 AM MDT)
**Format:** Video/voice call preferred  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Agenda:**

## 💡 Tips for Success (Organic Growth)

### General
- **Read your brief thoroughly** - It has everything you need
- **Over-communicate early** - Better to share too much than too little
- **Data-driven decisions** - When in doubt, look at the numbers
- **Bias toward action** - Don't wait for perfect, start and optimize
- **Ask for help** - Team is here to support each other
- **Be patient** - Organic growth compounds over weeks, not days

### Collaboration
- **Coordinate, don't duplicate** - Check what others are doing
- **Share wins publicly** - Celebrate team successes
- **Give constructive feedback** - Help each other improve
- **Respect deadlines** - Your delays impact others
- **Document everything** - Future you will thank you

### Mindset
- **Customer-first** - Every decision should benefit the customer
- **Test and learn** - Failure is data, not defeat
- **Long-term thinking** - Build brand, not just bookings
- **Utah-focused** - We serve a specific community
- **Tesla-specialist** - We're experts, not generalists
- **Community-driven** - Give value before asking for anything

### Organic-Specific
- **Be helpful, not salesy** - Communities reject spam
- **Build relationships** - Partnerships take time
- **Consistency compounds** - Daily effort > occasional bursts
- **Authenticity wins** - Real stories beat polished ads
- **Scarcity creates urgency** - Limited drops drive FOMO
- **UGC is gold** - Repost every customer mention
