## 📸 UGC AMPLIFICATION TACTICS

### In-Service Collection Process

**At Every Appointment:**
1. **Ask Permission Upfront:**
   - "Mind if we capture some content during the service? We'll blur your plate and tag you if you want."
   - Get verbal yes on phone recording (legal coverage)

2. **Capture Moments:**
   - Before: Photo of car, close-up of dirty coil
   - During: Customer doing normal activity (working, walking dog)
   - During: Gross coil reveal + customer reaction
   - After: Customer turning on AC, deep breath, smile

3. **Post-Service Handoff:**
   - AirDrop/text customer their photos + 15-second clip
   - Send caption templates (4 options in phone notes)
   - "Tag us @teslacoolpro + use #TeslaCoolPro for $10 off next service!"

### Weekly UGC Tasks

| Day | Task | Time Required |
|-----|------|---------------|
| Monday | Review tagged posts from weekend, like + comment on all | 15 min |
| Tuesday | Screenshot best UGC, add to "Customer Love" highlight | 10 min |
| Wednesday | DM customers who posted, thank them + remind of discount | 15 min |
| Thursday | Select UGC feature for Saturday post, get final permission | 10 min |
| Friday | Edit UGC compilation if using video | 30 min |
| Saturday | Post UGC spotlight, tag all featured customers | 15 min |
| Sunday | Track UGC entries for monthly contest | 10 min |

**Total Weekly Time:** ~1.5 hours

**Entry Requirements:**
- Tag @teslacoolpro
