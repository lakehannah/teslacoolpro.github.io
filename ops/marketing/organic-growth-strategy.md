## 🚀 Viral Content Framework

### The 3-Second Hook Rule

If you don't hook them in 3 seconds, they scroll. Period.

**Hook Formulas That Work:**

1. **Shock/Disgust:** "This is what's blowing into your lungs..." [gross coil reveal]
2. **Question:** "Why does your Tesla smell like old socks?" [relatable problem]
3. **Number/Cost:** "The $2,000 mistake Tesla owners make..." [curiosity + stakes]
4. **Transformation:** "Watch this disgusting AC become brand new..." [satisfying payoff]
5. **Secret/Insider:** "Tesla service centers don't want you to know this..." [conspiracy angle]
6. **ASMR/Satisfying:** [No words needed] [pressure washing sound, debris extraction]

### The Scroll-Stop Checklist

Every video must have at least 2 of these:
- [ ] Visual shock in first frame (gross coil, dramatic contrast)
- [ ] Text overlay with hook in first 2 seconds
- [ ] Movement/action immediately (no static shots)
- [ ] Sound that grabs attention (vacuum, spray, crunch)
- [ ] Relatable problem stated clearly
- [ ] Curiosity gap ("wait for it...")

## 📅 Weekly Content Rhythm

### Sunday: "Weekly Cooling Drop" 🎯

**Format:** 60-90 second reveal video  
**Timing:** 7-9pm Sunday (peak scroll time)  
**Purpose:** Flagship content, highest production value

**Structure:**
1. Hook (0-3s): "This week's grossest find..."
2. Setup (3-10s): Show the car, the problem, the owner's quote
3. Process montage (10-40s): Satisfying cleaning shots, ASMR moments
4. Reveal (40-55s): Before/after split screen, dramatic music swell
5. Reaction (55-70s): Customer's genuine reaction
6. CTA (70-90s): "Book your slot for next week - link in bio"

**Example Episodes:**
- Week 1: "Model Y Haven't Cleaned in 3 Years"
- Week 2: "Rideshare Driver 80K Miles Special"
- Week 3: "We Found a LEAF Inside the AC System"
- Week 4: "Allergy Mom's Life-Changing Clean"

**Cross-Platform Distribution:**
- TikTok (primary)
- Instagram Reels (same day)
- YouTube Shorts (same day, add to "Weekly Cooling Drop" playlist)
- Instagram Feed carousel (Monday morning, stills from video)
- Facebook (Tuesday, longer caption)
- Email newsletter (Wednesday, behind-the-scenes notes)

### Wednesday: ASMR Wednesday 🎧

**Format:** 30-60 second satisfying cleaning video  
**Audio:** Natural sounds amplified (vacuum, spray, air blast, debris falling)  
**Words:** Minimal or none

**Shots to Capture:**

- Debris being extracted (macro lens if possible)
- Dirty water draining out (gross but satisfying)
- Compressed air blowing out dust clouds
- Brush scrubbing coil fins (crunchy sounds)

**Caption:** "Turn your sound on 🔊 This is what clean sounds like."

## 📸 UGC Amplification System

### Make It EASY for Customers to Post

**In-Service Photo/Video Kit:**

1. **Pre-Service:**
   - "Mind if we take a quick photo of your car before we start? We'll blur your plate."
   - Get verbal consent on camera (for release purposes)

2. **During Service:**
   - Capture customer doing normal activities (working, walking dog, getting coffee)
   - Ask: "Want to see something gross?" Show them their coil, record reaction

3. **Post-Service:**
   - "Turn on your AC and take a deep breath. Can I capture that?"
   - Hand them phone: "Record yourself turning on the AC and tell me what you notice."

4. **Provide Ready-to-Post Assets:**

## 🚨 When Organic Isn't Working (Troubleshooting)

**Symptom: Low Views (<500/video after 2 weeks)**
- Hooks aren't stopping the scroll → Test 5 different hook styles
- Posting at wrong times → Check analytics, adjust schedule
- Content isn't shareable → Add more shock/value/emotion
- Not engaging with community → Spend 30 min/day commenting on others' content

**Symptom: Good Views, No Followers**
- Profile doesn't convert → Improve bio, highlight best content
- No clear niche → Emphasize "Tesla AC Specialists" in every post
- CTA missing → Add "Follow for more Tesla tips" to captions

**Symptom: Good Engagement, No Bookings**
- Link in bio not clear → Use Linktree with "BOOK NOW" as first button
- Pricing unclear → Add starting price to bio or highlight
- Service area confusion → List cities served prominently
- Trust gap → More testimonials, more before/afters, more face-of-brand content
