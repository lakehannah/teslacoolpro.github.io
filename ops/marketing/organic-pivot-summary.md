## 📱 Platform Strategy

### TikTok (Primary Focus - 40% of Effort)
**Why:** Highest organic reach potential, algorithm favors new accounts, viral ceiling unlimited

**Posting:** 1-2x/day minimum  
**Content Mix:**
- Weekly Cooling Drop (Sunday)
- ASMR Wednesday (satisfying sounds)
- Trending audio adaptations (2x/week)
- Educational quick hits (under 30s)
- Customer reactions (authentic moments)

**Growth Tactics:**
- Engage with Tesla community 30 min/day
- Comment on big accounts within 1 hour of their posts
- Use 8-12 hashtags (mix of large + niche)
- Stitch/duet viral Tesla content with expertise added
- Post when audience is active (check analytics)

### Instagram Reels (Secondary - 30% of Effort)
**Why:** Cross-post from TikTok with minor edits, feed carousels perform well, Stories build daily connection

**Posting:** 1x/day Reels + 3-5 Stories daily  
**Content Mix:**
- Same as TikTok (cross-posted)
- Educational carousels (save-worthy)
- Before/after feed posts
- Daily Stories (polls, Q&A, BTS)
- UGC features (Saturday spotlight)

**Growth Tactics:**
- Share every post to Stories with interactive sticker
- Engage with followers' content daily
- Use all interactive stickers (polls boost engagement)
- Build highlight reels (Education, Reviews, BTS, Offers)
- Collaborate with complementary accounts

### YouTube Shorts (Tertiary - 20% of Effort)
**Why:** Evergreen discovery, search-driven traffic, builds long-term library

**Posting:** 1x/day (same as Reels, cross-posted)  
**Content Mix:**
- Weekly Cooling Drop playlist
- Educational series (playlists by topic)
- ASMR collection
- Customer testimonial compilations

**Growth Tactics:**
- Optimize titles with keywords ("Tesla AC Cleaning Before After")
- Add #Shorts in title
- Link to full-length versions in description
- Create themed playlists (increases watch time)
- End screens pointing to booking page

### Facebook / LinkedIn (Remaining 10%)
**Why:** Facebook for community groups + older demographic; LinkedIn for fleet/B2B angle

**Posting:** 3-4x/week Facebook, 2-3x/week LinkedIn  
**Approach:** Repurpose Instagram content with platform-specific captions

## 🚨 Risk Mitigation

### If Organic Growth Is Slow (After 30 Days)

**Diagnosis Checklist:**
- [ ] Are hooks stopping the scroll in 3 seconds? (Test 5 different styles)
- [ ] Is content actually shareable? (Ask: "Would I send this to a friend?")
- [ ] Posting consistently daily? (No exceptions for 30 days)
- [ ] Engaging 30 min/day with community? (Not just broadcasting)
- [ ] Using trending audio within 48 hours? (Check Creative Center)
- [ ] Profile bio clear + CTA prominent? (Convert visitors to followers)

**Interventions:**
1. Audit top 10 performing posts in your niche - what hooks do they use?
2. Collaborate with micro-influencer (service exchange for content + exposure)
3. Run engagement pod with complementary businesses (mutual support)
4. Test controversial/take-a-stand content (polarizing = engaging)
5. Go live with Q&A session (algorithm boosts live video)

### If UGC Isn't Happening

**Diagnosis Checklist:**
- [ ] Are we asking IN THE MOMENT? (Right after they smell clean air)
- [ ] Are we sending assets within 1 hour? (Strike while iron is hot)
- [ ] Are caption templates truly copy-paste easy? (Remove all friction)
- [ ] Is incentive clearly communicated? ($10 off + contest entry)
- [ ] Are we featuring UGC prominently? (Show others it's rewarded)

**Interventions:**
1. Tech script update: "Want to see something gross? Can I record your reaction?"
2. Automate asset delivery via email template (send immediately after service)
3. Create physical handout card with QR code to caption templates
4. Announce first winner early (build FOMO for next month)
5. Feature "UGC of the Week" in Stories every Saturday (consistent recognition)
