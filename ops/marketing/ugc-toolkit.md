## 📸 Photo/Video Instructions for Customers

### "Want to Capture Your Own Content? Here's How:"

**Before Service:**
1. Open window slightly, turn on AC
2. Record yourself smelling it + making a face
3. Say: "This is what my AC smells like before [company] cleans it"

**During Service:**
1. Take photo of their van/setup
2. Quick video of tech working (ask permission first)
3. Capture yourself doing something normal (coffee, work, dog walk)

**After Service:**
1. Get in car, close doors/windows
2. Turn on AC to max
3. Record your face taking first deep breath
4. Say one sentence: "It smells like ______ now"

**Easy B-Roll They Can Capture:**
- Vent airflow (hold tissue up to it)
- Dashboard showing AC on
- Thumbs up from driver's seat
- Car + van in same shot (establishes mobile service)
- Screenshot of booking confirmation (social proof)

### When Customers Ask About Privacy

**Common Concerns & Responses:**

*"Can you blur my face too?"*
→ Absolutely! We can blur faces, focus only on hands/car, or shoot from angles that don't show you. Your comfort comes first.

*"I don't want to be on camera at all."*
→ No problem! We can photograph just the car and the work. Plenty of great content without people in it.

*"Can I review the post before it goes up?"*
→ Yes! Send us your handle and we'll DM you the draft before posting.

*"What if I change my mind later?"*
→ We'll remove the content within 24 hours of your request. Just DM us.

## 💡 Pro Tips for Maximum UGC

1. **Make the ask IN THE MOMENT** - Right after they smell clean air, they're most likely to post

2. **Show them the gross coil** - Shock value makes people want to share ("Look what was in MY car!")

3. **Give them ready-to-post assets** - Remove all friction. They shouldn't have to think.

4. **Respond to every tagged post within 1 hour** - Reinforces the behavior, builds relationship

5. **Feature UGC prominently** - When customers see others getting featured, they want in

6. **Make it about THEM, not you** - They're the hero of the story. You're the guide.

7. **Track who posts and reward consistently** - Word spreads when you actually deliver on incentives

8. **Create FOMO** - "Sarah got featured last week and got 200 new followers. You could be next!"

9. **Ask for specific platforms** - "We're trying to grow on TikTok this month - any chance you could post there?"

10. **Follow up without being annoying** - One reminder email is helpful. Three is spam.
