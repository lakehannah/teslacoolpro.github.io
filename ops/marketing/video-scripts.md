## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Equipment:**
- iPhone 15 Pro or similar (4K capability)
- Gimbal for smooth shots (DJI Osmo Mobile)
- Lapel mic for voiceovers (Rode Wireless Go)
- Portable LED light for dark garage shots

**Editing Software:**
- CapCut (mobile, free, easy)
- Final Cut Pro (desktop, advanced)
- Canva (graphics overlays)

**Brand Elements:**
- Consistent intro/outro (2 seconds max)
- Brand colors in text overlays (blue/white)
- Logo watermark bottom corner
- Same font family across all videos

**Best Practices:**
- Hook in first 3 seconds or they scroll
- Text on screen (many watch without sound)
- Keep under 60 seconds (algorithm preference)
- End with clear CTA
- Post when audience is active (check analytics)

**Trending Audio Strategy:**
- Check TikTok/Reels trending weekly
- Adapt scripts to fit popular sounds
- Put brand spin on trends (don't just copy)
- Original audio for educational content (builds authority)
