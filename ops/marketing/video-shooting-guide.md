## 🎬 Shot List Templates

### Weekly Cooling Drop (90 seconds)

**Pre-Service Shots:**
- [ ] Car pulling in / parked at location (wide shot)
- [ ] Customer greeting + brief interview (medium shot)
  - "How long since AC was cleaned?"
  - "What are you noticing with the smell/airflow?"
- [ ] License plate blur setup (do this BEFORE filming)
- [ ] Initial coil inspection reveal (close-up, dramatic)

**During Service Shots:**
- [ ] Van arrival + setup time-lapse (wide, sped up)
- [ ] Equipment unpacking (quick cuts, 2-3 seconds each)
- [ ] Spray on coil close-up (macro if possible)
- [ ] Debris extraction (satisfying pull-out moments)
- [ ] Customer doing activity while waiting (candid)
- [ ] Dirty water draining (gross but satisfying)
- [ ] Brush scrubbing action (close-up)
- [ ] Compressed air blast (slow-mo if possible)

**Post-Service Shots:**
- [ ] Clean coil reveal (same angle as dirty for comparison)
- [ ] Before/after split screen setup (shoot both same way)
- [ ] Customer getting back in car (anticipation)
- [ ] Customer turning on AC (capture face, not just hands)
- [ ] Deep breath + reaction (the money shot)
- [ ] Quick testimonial question:
  - "What do you notice?"
  - "How long has it been since it smelled this clean?"
- [ ] Handshake/thank you moment
- [ ] Van driving away (closing shot)

**B-Roll to Capture (Always Get These):**
- [ ] Hands working (gloves on, tools in action)
- [ ] Tool close-ups (branding visible if possible)
- [ ] Tesla badge/logo on car (establishes brand)
- [ ] Interior vent shots (where air comes out)
- [ ] Dashboard AC controls (relatable interface)
- [ ] Neighborhood/location context (establishes mobile service)

### ASMR Video (30-60 seconds, no talking)

**Required Shots (focus on sound):**

1. **Opening (0-3 seconds):**
   - Close-up of dirty coil
   - Text overlay: "Turn sound on 🔊"

2. **Spray Sequence (3-15 seconds):**
   - Macro shot of spray hitting coil
   - Capture the hiss + liquid contact sound
   - Show dirt beginning to loosen

3. **Extraction Sequence (15-30 seconds):**
   - Debris being pulled/brushed out
   - Crunchy brushing sounds
   - Visible dirt falling/dripping

4. **Drain Sequence (30-45 seconds):**
   - Dirty water flowing out
   - Glug/gurgle sounds
   - Show volume of contamination

5. **Air Blast Sequence (45-55 seconds):**
   - Compressed air blowing dust cloud
   - Whoosh sound
   - Slow-mo if possible

6. **Final Reveal (55-60 seconds):**
   - Clean coil gleaming
   - Satisfying "ahhh" moment
   - Text: "Clean. 🔊"

**ASMR Audio Tips:**
- Record in quiet environment (no wind, no traffic)
- Get mic as close to action as possible
- Use windscreen on mic outdoors
- Record 10 seconds of "room tone" for editing buffer
- Don't add music - let natural sounds shine

---

### Customer Reaction Video (30 seconds)

**Shot Sequence:**

1. **Setup (0-5 seconds):**
   - Customer standing by car
   - Quick intro: "This is [Name], just had their AC cleaned"

2. **Before Question (5-15 seconds):**
   - "What was your AC like before?"
   - Let them describe the problem
   - Capture facial expressions

3. **The Moment (15-25 seconds):**
   - Customer gets in car
   - Turns on AC (capture hand on control)
   - Camera inside car on their face
   - Wait for genuine reaction (don't rush)
   - Deep breath, smile, surprise

4. **After Question (25-30 seconds):**
   - "What do you notice now?"
   - One-sentence answer
   - Natural, not scripted

**Director Tips:**
- Don't over-direct. Authenticity > perfection.
- Ask open-ended questions ("What do you notice?" not "Does it smell better?")
- Give them a moment to actually experience it before asking
- If they stumble on words, that's fine - it's real
- Capture silence/reaction, not just words

## 🎥 Camera Settings

### iPhone Settings (Recommended)

**Video Resolution:**
- 4K at 30fps (best quality, manageable file size)
- 1080p at 60fps (if storage is concern, smoother motion)

**Grid Lines:** ON (for composition)

**HDR Video:** ON (better dynamic range in bright sun)

**Cinematic Mode:** Use sparingly (good for reactions, not process)

**Exposure Lock:** Tap and hold on subject, slide down slightly (prevents blown highlights)

**Focus Lock:** Same as above (tap and hold)

---

### Android Settings (Samsung/Pixel)

**Video Resolution:**
- 4K UHD at 30fps
- FHD at 60fps alternative

**HDR10+:** ON if available

**Stabilization:** ON (but still use gimbal for best results)

**Scene Optimizer:** OFF (you want natural colors)

## 💡 Lighting Guidelines

### Outdoor/Natural Light

**Best Times:**
- Golden hour: 1 hour after sunrise, 1 hour before sunset
- Overcast days: Soft, even light (actually ideal)

**Avoid:**
- Harsh noon sun (creates harsh shadows)
- Direct sun on shiny car surfaces (glare city)
- Backlit shots (subject becomes silhouette)

**Quick Fixes:**
- Move car into shade if possible
- Use portable LED to fill shadows on faces
- Bounce light off white surface (car, wall, reflector)

### Indoor/Garage Lighting

**Setup:**
- Portable LED on stand, 45-degree angle to subject
- Second light (or phone flashlight) as fill from opposite side
- Avoid single overhead light (creates raccoon eyes)

**Settings:**
- LED at 50% brightness (adjust as needed)
- Color temperature: Daylight (5600K) matches most garage lights

## 🎤 Audio Best Practices

### Lapel Mic Placement

**On Customer:**
- Clip to shirt collar, 6 inches below chin
- Run wire under shirt (cleaner look)
- Test levels before recording ("Can you hear me okay?")

**On Technician:**
- Same placement
- Keep transmitter in pocket, clip to waistband

**Wind Protection:**
- Use foam windscreen outdoors
- Position behind car/wall when possible
- Avoid filming in high wind if possible

### Phone Built-In Mic (If No Lapel)

**Positioning:**
- Get phone within 3 feet of speaker
- Point bottom of phone (mic location) toward subject
- Don't cover mic with fingers

**Environment:**
- Turn off nearby fans/AC if possible
- Close car windows
- Wait for quiet moments between traffic

## 📐 Composition Rules

### Rule of Thirds

Enable grid lines. Place subjects at intersection points:
- Eyes on top third line (for faces)
- Horizon on top or bottom third (not center)
- Coil/action in center third (for process shots)

### Leading Lines

Use natural lines to guide eye:
- Car body lines pointing to subject
- Hose/cable leading to action
- Vent slats directing to airflow

### Depth

Create layers in shot:
- Foreground: Part of car, tool, arm
- Middle: Main action/subject
- Background: Blurred context (neighborhood, van)

### Close-Ups Matter

Get THREE distances for every scene:
- Wide: Establish location/context
- Medium: Show action/process
- Close-up: Detail/emotion (the money shots)

## 🎬 Filming Workflow

### Pre-Service Checklist (5 minutes)

- [ ] Phone charged + memory cleared (at least 5GB free)
- [ ] Gimbal balanced + charged
- [ ] Mic connected + tested
- [ ] Lens wiped clean (fingerprint check)
- [ ] Settings confirmed (4K 30fps, HDR on)
- [ ] Customer permission obtained (verbal on recording)
- [ ] License plate blur plan decided

### During Service Filming

**Technician Role:**
- Focus on quality work first
- Pause briefly for key shots (spray, extraction, reveal)
- Narrate what you're doing (for voiceover later)
- Stay aware of camera position (don't block with body)

**Camera Operator Role:**
- Capture everything on shot list
- Get multiple angles of key moments
- Record 5 seconds extra on each shot (for editing buffer)
- Watch audio levels (red = bad)
- Communicate quietly with tech ("One more second, got it")

### Post-Service Wrap (2 minutes)

- [ ] Confirm all shots captured
- [ ] Get customer testimonial on camera
- [ ] Film clean coil from same angle as dirty
- [ ] Capture customer reaction
- [ ] Thank customer for being on camera
- [ ] Backup footage immediately (cloud or laptop)

## ✂️ Quick Editing Guide (CapCut)

### Import + Organize (5 minutes)

1. Open CapCut, create new project
2. Import all footage from camera roll
3. Create folders: "Before," "During," "After," "B-Roll"
4. Watch all footage, star favorites (heart icon)

### Rough Cut (15 minutes)

1. Add starred clips to timeline in order
2. Trim each clip to essential moments only
3. Remove dead space (pause before/after action)
4. Target length: 90 seconds for Weekly Drop

### Fine Cut (15 minutes)

1. Add transitions between major sections (simple fade, 0.3s)
2. Adjust clip speeds (time-lapse sections, slow-mo reveals)
3. Add text overlays (hooks, explanations, CTAs)
4. Sync cuts to music beats (if using music)

### Audio Mix (10 minutes)

1. Add background music (lower volume, -20dB)
2. Boost dialogue clips (+5dB)
3. Enhance ASMR sounds (normalize, +3dB)
4. Add sound effects if needed (subtle whooshes, dings)
5. Fade out music at end (1 second)

### Color + Polish (10 minutes)

1. Apply filter consistently across all clips (subtle)
2. Adjust exposure on dark shots (+10%)
3. Increase saturation slightly (+5%)
4. Sharpen close-ups (+10%)
5. Add vignette for cinematic feel (subtle)

### Export Settings

- Resolution: 1080x1920 (vertical for TikTok/Reels)
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Quality: High (not highest - file size vs. platform compression)
- Format: MP4 (H.264)

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Length: 15-90 seconds (sweet spot: 30-60s)

- Caption: First line is hook (only line visible in feed)

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Length: 15-90 seconds
- Cover image: Select frame with text overlay
- Caption: Use all 2,200 characters if needed

### YouTube Shorts

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Length: Under 60 seconds (strict limit)
- Title: Include #Shorts in title
- Description: Link to full version if applicable

- Aspect ratio: 4:5 vertical (1080x1350)
- Slides: 3-10 images
- First slide: Hook image (before/after split)

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Filming Mistakes

❌ **Shaky footage** → Use gimbal, always  
❌ **Bad audio** → Test before recording, use lapel mic  
❌ **Wrong orientation** → Vertical (9:16) for short-form, always  
❌ **No variety in shots** → Get wide, medium, close-up of everything  
❌ **Forgetting to blur plates** → Do it before posting, legal requirement  
❌ **Over-directing customers** → Authentic > polished  

### Editing Mistakes

❌ **Too long** → Cut ruthlessly. If it doesn't serve the story, remove it.  
❌ **Music too loud** → Dialogue and natural sounds should be audible  
❌ **Too many effects** → Simple transitions, subtle filters  
❌ **No hook in first 3 seconds** → Front-load the interesting part  
❌ **Inconsistent branding** → Same font, colors, watermark on all videos  
❌ **No CTA** → Always end with clear next step  

### Posting Mistakes

❌ **Wrong caption format** → First line must hook (it's all people see)  
❌ **No hashtags** → Use 8-12 relevant tags  
❌ **Posting then ghosting** → Engage for 2 hours after posting  
❌ **Ignoring comments** → Reply to every comment, especially early ones  
❌ **Not sharing to stories** → Amplify every post with story share + sticker  
❌ **Inconsistent posting** → Daily minimum, no exceptions
