## CAPTION

**Headline:** Want to save $2,000? Read this. 💰

**Body:**
Let's talk about the most expensive mistake we see Tesla owners make.

They ignore their AC until it stops working.

Here's the progression:

**Year 1-2:** Slight musty smell when AC turns on
↳ "It'll go away" (it won't)

**Year 2-3:** Smell gets worse, airflow decreases
↳ "I'll book a cleaning soon" (you don't)

**Year 3-4:** AC blows weak, smells terrible, coils start corroding
↳ "Maybe it's just the heat?" (it's not)

**Year 4+:** AC fails completely. Evaporator coil is shot.
↳ Dealership quote: $1,500-2,500 for replacement 😭

**The math is simple:**
- Regular cleaning: $250 every 1-2 years
- Neglect + replacement: $2,000+ once

We've seen coils so corroded they crumbled during inspection. We've also seen coils that looked brand new after 3 years of annual cleanings.

**The difference?** Maintenance.

Your Tesla's evaporator coil is basically a radiator that gets cold. When warm, humid air hits it, condensation forms. That moisture + dust + pollen = mold food.

Clean it regularly? No problem.
Ignore it? Pay the price.
