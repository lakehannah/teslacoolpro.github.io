# Tesla Cool Pro - Paid Ads Strategy

**Prepared:** June 25, 2026  
**Market:** Utah County + Salt Lake County, Utah  
**Service:** Mobile Tesla AC & Radiator Cleaning ($250-$450 per service)

---

## Executive Summary

Tesla Cool Pro serves a niche but growing market: Tesla owners in Utah who need specialized AC/radiator maintenance. With 500+ Teslas already serviced and strong word-of-mouth (4.9★ rating), paid ads should accelerate direct bookings while building brand awareness in Utah's rapidly growing EV community.

**Key Insight:** Utah EV adoption is accelerating fast—EVs (mainly Teslas) are "swarming" Utah County and Salt Lake City, with EVs reaching 10% of new car sales in 2024 and growing. High gas prices in 2026 are pushing more Utahns toward EVs.

## Customer Journey Mapping

### Stage 1: Awareness (Problem Recognition)
**Customer State:** "My AC isn't blowing cold" / "Why is my range dropping?" / "Fans are loud at stops"
- **Channels:** Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Search (problem-based queries)
- **Content:** Educational posts about hidden radiator problems, warning signs
- **Goal:** Make them aware the problem exists AND that you specialize in fixing it

### Stage 2: Consideration (Solution Research)
**Customer State:** "Tesla radiator cleaning near me" / "Mobile Tesla AC service Utah" / "Tesla Cool Pro reviews"
- **Channels:** Google Search (intent-based), Meta retargeting, Instagram
- **Content:** Service comparisons, before/after photos, testimonials, pricing transparency
- **Goal:** Position Tesla Cool Pro as the obvious choice vs. dealership ($1,000+ savings)

### Stage 3: Booking (Conversion)
**Customer State:** Ready to book, comparing options
- **Channels:** Google Search (branded), direct website visits, retargeting ads
- **Content:** Flash sale offers, BOGO deals, urgency triggers ("Free Mobile Service!")
- **Goal:** Remove friction, create urgency, drive to payment.html

### Stage 4: Retention & Referral
**Customer State:** Post-service, satisfied customer
- **Channels:** Email/SMS follow-up, Meta custom audiences (customer lists)
- **Content:** Maintenance reminders, referral incentives, seasonal checkups
- **Goal:** Repeat bookings (BOGO second visit), referrals, reviews

## Platform Recommendations

### Primary: Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)
**Why:** Tesla owners are highly active on social, visual platform perfect for before/after content, precise targeting available

**Budget Allocation:** 60% of total ad spend  
**Initial Monthly Budget:** $1,500-$2,500

**Targeting Strategy:**
- **Location:** Utah County + Salt Lake County (25-mile radius around Lindon/Orem)
- **Interests:** Tesla Motors, Tesla Model 3, Tesla Model Y, Electric vehicles, Elon Musk
- **Behaviors:** Engaged shoppers, luxury vehicle owners
- **Custom Audiences:** 
  - Website visitors (pixel required)
  - Video viewers (50%+ of ad views)
  - Customer email lists (for retention/referral campaigns)
- **Lookalike Audiences:** 1% lookalike of past customers (once pixel has 500+ conversions)

**Ad Formats:**
- Carousel ads (before/after sequence)
- Video ads (15-30 sec process demos)
- Collection ads (mobile-first shopping experience)
- Lead gen forms (for free inspection bookings)

### Secondary: Google Ads (Search + Local)
**Why:** Captures high-intent searches from people actively looking for solutions

**Budget Allocation:** 35% of total ad spend  
**Initial Monthly Budget:** $900-$1,500

**Keyword Strategy:**
- **High Intent:** "Tesla radiator cleaning," "Tesla AC repair Utah," "mobile Tesla service near me"
- **Problem-Based:** "Tesla AC not cold," "Tesla overheating," "Tesla range dropping"
- **Branded:** "Tesla Cool Pro," "Tesla Cool Pro reviews"
- **Competitor:** "Tesla service center radiator," "Tesla dealership AC repair"

**Ad Extensions:**
- Call extensions (click-to-call)
- Location extensions (service area)
- Sitelink extensions (pricing, testimonials, booking)
- Review extensions (4.9★ rating)

### Tertiary: TikTok Ads (Test Phase)
**Why:** Growing EV enthusiast community, viral potential for satisfying before/after content

**Budget Allocation:** 5% of total ad spend (test budget)  
**Initial Monthly Budget:** $100-$300

**Content Strategy:**
- Satisfying cleaning videos (pressure washing radiators)
- "Things your Tesla dealer won't tell you" educational series
- Behind-the-scenes mobile service setup
- Customer reaction videos

## Tracking & Conversion Measurement

### Essential Setup (Week 1 Priority)

**1. Meta Pixel + Conversions API**
- Install on website (all pages)
- Track events: PageView, ViewContent (pricing page), InitiateCheckout (payment.html), Purchase (confirmation)
- Set up Aggregated Event Measurement for iOS 14+ compliance
- Create custom conversions for "Booked Appointment" and "Completed Payment"

**2. Google Ads Conversion Tracking**
- Install Google tag (gtag.js) on all pages
- Track: Form submissions, phone calls (click-to-call), payment completions
- Import offline conversions if bookings happen via phone/text
- Enable enhanced conversions for better attribution

**3. Call Tracking**
- Use unique phone numbers per ad platform (e.g., CallRail, WhatConverts)
- Track call duration, recordings, source attribution
- Set up text message tracking for "COOL" keyword

**4. UTM Parameters**
- All ads must use UTM tagging: `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=summer-flash-sale&utm_content=ad-variation-1`
- Create consistent naming convention across all platforms
- Document in tracking spreadsheet

**5. Google Analytics 4 (GA4)**
- Set up GA4 property if not exists
- Configure conversion events matching ad platforms
- Create audience segments for retargeting
- Set up funnel visualization (landing → pricing → payment → confirmation)

## A/B Testing Plan

### Week 1-2: Creative Testing
- **Test:** Before/after static images vs. video time-lapse
- **Hypothesis:** Video will have 2x engagement, but static may convert better for direct response
- **Metric:** CTR, landing page conversion rate

### Week 3-4: Headline Testing
- **Test:** Problem-aware ("AC Struggling?") vs. savings-focused ("Save $1,000+")
- **Hypothesis:** Savings headlines will outperform for cold audiences
- **Metric:** CTR, CPC, conversion rate

### Week 5-6: Offer Testing
- **Test:** $250 Standard Clean vs. BOGO $450 for 2
- **Hypothesis:** BOGO will have higher AOV but lower conversion rate
- **Metric:** CPA, ROAS, average order value

### Week 7-8: Audience Testing
- **Test:** Interest-based (Tesla owners) vs. lookalike (customer list)
- **Hypothesis:** Lookalike will have 30% lower CPA once pixel matures
- **Metric:** CPA, ROAS, frequency

## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Risks
1. **Ad Account Restrictions:** Meta/Google may flag "Tesla" trademark usage
   - **Mitigation:** Use "Tesla-compatible" or "For Tesla Vehicles" language; avoid implying official partnership
   
2. **Seasonal Demand Drop:** Winter months may see 50%+ lower demand
   - **Mitigation:** Shift messaging to preventive maintenance, BOGO deals for spring prep

3. **Creative Fatigue:** Audiences may tire of same ads after 2-3 weeks
   - **Mitigation:** Maintain creative pipeline, test new angles monthly

4. **Tracking Gaps:** iOS privacy changes may underreport conversions
   - **Mitigation:** Use Conversions API, offline conversion uploads, phone tracking

5. **Competitor Response:** Dealerships or new mobile services may undercut pricing
   - **Mitigation:** Emphasize specialization, expertise, and 500+ serviced track record
