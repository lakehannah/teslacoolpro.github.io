**Body:**
Ever wonder what's actually happening when you press that AC button in your Tesla?

Let's break it down (without the engineering degree):

**1️⃣ Air Intake**
Your Tesla pulls air from outside (or recirculates cabin air). That air contains dust, pollen, pollution, and humidity.

**2️⃣ Cabin Filter**
First line of defense. Catches big particles. Needs replacing every 12-24 months. (Yes, you can DIY this one.)

**3️⃣ Evaporator Coil** ⭐ THE IMPORTANT PART
This is where the magic (and problems) happen. The coil gets COLD. When warm air hits it, condensation forms. 

Think of a cold soda can on a hot day - that water on the outside? Same physics.

Now imagine dust + moisture = mold food. 🍄

**4️⃣ Blower Motor**
Pushes the cooled air through your vents. If the coil is clogged, this works harder = shorter lifespan.

**5️⃣ Your Face** 
Hopefully with clean, cool, odor-free air. If not... see step 3.

**Why we're obsessed with the evaporator coil:**

It's the heart of your AC system. It's also the most expensive part to replace ($1,500-2,500). And it's completely hidden behind the dashboard.

Out of sight, out of mind... until it fails.
