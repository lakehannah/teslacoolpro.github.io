## VISUAL DESCRIPTION

**Video Concept: "Your Time vs. Our Time"**

**Scene 1 (0-3 sec):** 
- Hook text on screen: "Dealership AC service:"
- Quick cuts: Person waiting in lobby, looking at watch, boring magazines, coffee refills
- Clock spinning rapidly (time-lapse effect)
- Text: "3-4 hours"

**Scene 2 (4-7 sec):**
- Transition swipe
- Hook text: "Tesla Cool Pro service:"
- Technician arrives in branded van at home/office
- Customer barely looks up from laptop/coffee
- Text: "We come to YOU"

**Scene 3 (8-25 sec):**
- Time-lapse of service:
  - Van pulls up (5 sec)
  - Quick setup (3 sec)
  - Working on car while owner works/walks dog/gets mail (10 sec)
  - Pack up (3 sec)
  - Handshake with happy customer (4 sec)

**Scene 4 (26-35 sec):**
- Customer gets in car, turns on AC
- Visible relief/smile
- Text: "Cold air. No smell. Zero hassle."

**Scene 5 (36-45 sec):**
- End card with branding
- Text: "Book your mobile service"
- Website / phone number
- "First-time: 20% off"

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Equipment needed:**
- Smartphone or mirrorless camera
- Tripod / gimbal for smooth shots
- Lapel mic for any voiceover
- Good natural lighting (morning/afternoon)

**B-roll to capture:**
- Van arriving from multiple angles
- Hands working (gloves, tools)
- Customer doing normal activities (candid)
- Close-ups of equipment setup
- Happy customer reactions (get permission/releases)

**Editing style:**
- Quick cuts (keep energy high)
- Text overlays for key points
- Timer graphic for service duration
- Brand colors in transitions
