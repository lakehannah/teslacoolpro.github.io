1. Open Facebook app
2. Go to each Tesla group
3. Search "AC" or "radiator" in group
4. Sort by "Recent" (last 24-48 hours)

**Evening (5-7 min):**
1. Repeat search with different keyword ("fan", "overheating")
2. Respond to 2-3 more posts
3. Engage with any replies you got from morning

**Weekly (30 min on Sunday):**
1. Join 2-3 new Tesla groups
2. Review what search terms got best responses
3. Adjust templates based on what's working
